=== BAGIAN A: VENDOR BILL ===

Model VendorBill dan VendorBillItem sudah ada di schema.
Tambahkan VendorBillModule atau masukkan ke PurchasingModule.

Endpoint yang harus ada:
GET    /api/purchasing/bills
GET    /api/purchasing/bills/:id
POST   /api/purchasing/bills                    (buat bill manual atau dari GR)
PUT    /api/purchasing/bills/:id
DELETE /api/purchasing/bills/:id                (hanya status draft)
POST   /api/purchasing/bills/from-gr/:grId      (generate bill dari Goods Receipt)
POST   /api/purchasing/bills/:id/payments       (catat pembayaran, bisa partial)
GET    /api/purchasing/bills/:id/payments       (riwayat pembayaran)
POST   /api/purchasing/bills/:id/approve
GET    /api/purchasing/bills/aging              (AP aging: 0-30, 31-60, 61-90, >90)
GET    /api/purchasing/bills/:id/pdf
POST   /api/purchasing/bills/import
GET    /api/purchasing/bills/import-template

Status flow VendorBill: draft → submitted → approved → partial_paid → paid → overdue → cancelled

Auto-jurnal saat bill dibuat (status approved):
DEBIT  : akun Persediaan atau akun Beban (tergantung tipe produk)
KREDIT : akun Hutang Usaha

Auto-jurnal saat bill dibayar:
DEBIT  : akun Hutang Usaha
KREDIT : akun Kas/Bank

=== BAGIAN B: 3-WAY MATCHING ===

Tambahkan logika validasi 3-way matching di VendorBillService:

method: validateThreeWayMatch(billId: string)
- Ambil VendorBill → ambil PurchaseOrder terkait → ambil GoodsReceipt terkait
- Bandingkan: qty di PO, qty diterima di GR, qty di Bill
- Return: { matched: boolean, discrepancies: [{ productId, poQty, grQty, billQty }] }

Endpoint: GET /api/purchasing/bills/:id/three-way-match

=== BAGIAN C: LANDED COST ===

Model LandedCost dan LandedCostItem sudah ada di schema.
Pastikan endpoint ini berfungsi:

GET    /api/purchasing/landed-costs
POST   /api/purchasing/landed-costs
GET    /api/purchasing/landed-costs/:id
PUT    /api/purchasing/landed-costs/:id
POST   /api/purchasing/landed-costs/:id/validate  (apply ke nilai stok)

Metode alokasi landed cost:
- by_qty: biaya dibagi rata berdasarkan jumlah unit
- by_value: biaya dialokasikan proporsional berdasarkan nilai produk
- by_weight: berdasarkan berat produk

Saat validate:
- Tambahkan biaya landed cost ke nilai stok produk (update StockValuation)
- Buat jurnal: DEBIT Persediaan, KREDIT Kas/Hutang
